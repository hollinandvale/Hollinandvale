About Hollin & Vale

Comfort, considered.

A contemporary UK furniture brand built around one principle: fewer pieces, better chosen.

A focused point of view.

Our collection brings together British-made sofas, armchairs and upholstered seating with distinct silhouettes and a calm, considered character. From relaxed loose covers to more tailored forms, each design offers a different way to make a room feel like home.

The upholstery in our collection is handmade to order in Lancashire by specialist makers, using solid hardwood frames and sprung bases. Choose your size, fabric and colour, see the published retail price, and ask us to help with the details. Delivery is included to serviceable Great Britain mainland addresses; postcode and access are confirmed before ordering.
