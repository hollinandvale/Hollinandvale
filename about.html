About Hollin & Vale

Comfort, considered.

A contemporary UK furniture brand being built around one principle: fewer pieces, better chosen.

A focused point of view.

Our launch collection is centred on sofas, armchairs and upholstered seating with strong silhouettes, genuine comfort and dependable manufacturing. We are deliberately building a curated range rather than filling the site with unrelated products.

The same design language will carry across residential customers and Hollin & Vale Trade, allowing the brand to serve homes, hospitality, leisure and workplace environments with a consistent identity.

Curated

Every piece should feel as though it belongs in the same collection.

Comfort-led

Proportion, support, upholstery and usability matter as much as appearance.

Built to grow

Launching sofa-first, with room to develop armchairs, modular seating and wider furniture categories.

Brand palette

Warm, architectural and tactile.
