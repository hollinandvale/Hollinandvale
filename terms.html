Website & ordering information

Clear from the start.

Retail prices, delivery and the next steps when choosing furniture with Hollin & Vale.

Last updated: 4 November 2026.

Using this website

This website presents our retail furniture collection and supports order requests, product advice, trade enquiries and supplier introductions. It does not currently offer checkout or take payment. Submitting an enquiry or sending an email does not create an accepted order.

Retail prices and your selection

Published retail prices are the total product price for the selected standard configuration and include the delivery described below. Choose the size and fabric to see the corresponding price. Your colour, specification, delivery postcode and access are confirmed before acceptance. Any separately requested service or delivery outside the included area must be agreed and priced before you commit.

Included delivery

Delivery included to serviceable Great Britain mainland addresses. Islands and some AB, IV and PA locations need a separate quote; confirm postcode and access before ordering.

The exact delivery service, estimated timing and any access requirements will be confirmed for your order. Your written confirmation states any assembly or packaging service included. Removal of existing furniture is a separate arrangement unless expressly agreed.

Order acceptance and payment

After your request, we confirm the chosen furniture, specification, total price, delivery arrangements, expected timing, payment options and full order terms in writing before accepting an order. Finance is not currently available. An order request is not a payment or an order acceptance.

Product information

Dimensions are manufacturer-published guides for handmade furniture and can vary slightly. Photographs show example configurations; size, colour and upholstery affect the finished appearance. Screen colours may differ from physical fabric. Check the final specification and delivery access before ordering.

Consumer cancellation and returns

For a standard purchase made at a distance, you may cancel before delivery and up to 14 days after receiving the furniture. Notify Hollin & Vale clearly, for example by emailing enquiries@hollinandvale.co.uk. You then have 14 days to return it.

Choosing standard catalogue sizes, fabrics or colours does not automatically remove cancellation rights because production is made to order. A genuinely bespoke or personalised specification may be exempt; any exemption will be explained before the contract is made.

Contact us for return instructions. The return address, practical arrangements and any direct return cost you must pay, including the cost or estimate for bulky furniture, will be provided before a contract is agreed. If the required return-cost information was not supplied before the contract, we will bear those costs.

Refunds include the standard delivery element of the price; we will not subtract the cost of included standard delivery. They are made without undue delay and within 14 days of receiving the furniture back or, if earlier, proof that you sent it back. We may make a lawful deduction for loss of value caused by handling beyond what is necessary to inspect the furniture, but do not charge a cancellation or restocking fee for exercising a legal right to cancel.

Faults and aftercare

If furniture is faulty, damaged or not as described, contact Hollin & Vale. Your statutory rights apply independently of any manufacturer’s guarantee. We handle your customer enquiry and arrange the appropriate remedy; you are not required to resolve it only with the manufacturer. Where goods must be returned because they are faulty or misdescribed, the necessary reasonable return costs are not your responsibility.

The manufacturer’s five-year structural guarantee covers frames, springing and webbing subject to its applicable terms and exclusions, including normal wear, misuse and neglect. Written coverage is provided before order acceptance. See guarantee and aftercare information.

Trade orders

Businesses can enquire about trade or project pricing. Any proposal depends on quantities, specification and delivery requirements. Business order terms are agreed separately. Consumer cancellation provisions apply to consumers rather than purchases made for business purposes.

Intellectual property

Our original written content, visual identity and site design may not be commercially reused without permission, except where the law allows. Product photography is supplied through our approved manufacturer’s trade library for promoting the corresponding furniture. Rights remain with the respective owner.

External services and your rights

The website uses third-party hosting, email and domain services. Nothing here limits rights or liability that cannot lawfully be limited. These website terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales, without removing mandatory consumer protections that apply to you.
