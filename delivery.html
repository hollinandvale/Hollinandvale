Built with reassurance

Five-year structural guarantee.

The manufacturer provides a five-year structural and manufacturing guarantee covering frames, springing and webbing, subject to its terms. It does not cover every part of the furniture.

Normal wear and tear, misuse and neglect are excluded. We provide the applicable written guarantee and any further conditions before accepting your order.

Hollin & Vale is your point of contact if you need help or want to report a problem. Email enquiries@hollinandvale.co.uk with your order details and a description of the issue. The guarantee is additional to, and does not affect, your statutory rights.

Looking after your furniture

A little regular care.

Vacuum upholstery gently with a soft brush attachment, plump cushions regularly and rotate loose seat cushions to encourage even wear. Avoid prolonged direct sunlight.

Alder has a removable washable cover. Follow the fabric care label supplied with your furniture before washing. Fixed covers must not be treated as removable or machine washable.

Screen colours can differ from the actual fabric. Ask us about swatches and fabric care before making your final choice.

Questions after delivery?

Contact Hollin & Vale about any fault, damage or concern with your order. For standard distance purchases, consumers can normally tell us they wish to cancel within 14 days after delivery, then have a further 14 days to return the furniture.

Made-to-order production does not automatically make a standard size, fabric or colour selection bespoke. Any genuinely personalised specification and its effect on cancellation rights will be explained before acceptance.
