What to expect when choosing made-to-order furniture with Hollin & Vale.

Made for your order.

Our collection is handmade to order in Lancashire. Choose your model, size and upholstery, then request a quotation so the details can be agreed before you commit.

Estimated timingAllow approximately 6–8 weeks for production, followed by 1–2 weeks for delivery. Material availability, your specification and destination can affect timing; the estimate for your order is confirmed in your quotation.
Delivery to your addressWhite-glove delivery can be arranged, subject to your postcode and access. Delivery charges and the exact service included will be set out in your quotation. Some remote areas and islands need separate confirmation.
Check the route into your roomMeasure doorways, halls, stairs, lifts and the final position. Share any tight turns, restricted parking or access concerns before your order is confirmed.
Looking after your furniture

A little regular care.

Vacuum upholstery gently with a soft brush attachment, plump cushions regularly and rotate loose seat cushions to encourage even wear. Avoid prolonged direct sunlight.

Raffles has a removable washable cover. Always follow the care label for the selected fabric; do not assume every upholstery fabric or fixed cover can be washed or removed.

Colours on a screen can vary from the fabric itself. Ask us about swatches and fabric care before making your final choice.

Structural support

Understand the cover.

The manufacturer advertises a five-year structural and manufacturing guarantee covering frames, springing and webbing. This is not five-year cover for every part of the furniture.

Normal wear and tear, misuse and neglect are excluded under the manufacturer’s terms. We will confirm the applicable written guarantee, any further conditions and how to report an issue with your quotation.
