The Hollin & Vale choosing guide

Make room
for the right sofa.

A little measuring and a closer look at fabric make the choice easier. Start with your room, compare the seat, then check the whole route into your home.

01 · Start with the room

Measure the shape,
not just the wall.

A three-seater is a size label, not a standard width. The Marlow, Calder and Alder three-seaters are 185cm wide; Rowan’s is 205cm. Check the exact design and size.

Compare the three-seaters

  1. Mark out the footprint.

    Measure overall width and depth, then mark them on the floor with removable tape or paper. Check that doors, drawers, radiators and the route through the room still work.

  2. Compare the seat.

    Measure a sofa you already use: the height from floor to seat, the usable seat depth and the width between the arms. Compare these with the selected design. Overall depth and seat depth describe different things.

  3. Think about daily use.

    Consider who will use the sofa, where side tables will go and how much floor space you want to leave around it. A washable loose cover and a fixed cover have different care routines.

02 · Check the journey in

From the street to its final place.

A sofa that fits the room may still meet a tight turn on the way in. Share any access concerns before ordering so the delivery arrangements and final specification can be checked.

Measure every narrow point.

Record the clear opening width and height of entrances and internal doorways, hallway widths, tight corners, stair widths and landings. For a lift, measure both its doorway and internal space, and check any building restrictions.

Allow for door handles, banisters, low ceilings and other fixed obstructions. Product width alone does not confirm that a sofa can pass through a doorway or turn on a landing.

Tell us about the address.

Share the delivery postcode, floor, stairs or lift, restricted parking and the route into the intended room. Photos and a simple sketch can help explain a difficult turn.

Ask us to confirm packed dimensions and whether any legs or other parts are removable for your chosen model. Do not assume a sofa can be dismantled or that assembly, packaging removal or old-sofa removal is included.

See fabric in your own light.

Ask about physical swatch availability for your chosen colours. If samples are available, compare them in the room in daylight and in the lighting you use in the evening, alongside your existing finishes.

Sample availability, any charge or postage, and timing are confirmed with your enquiry.

Understand the care routine.

Vacuum upholstery gently with a soft brush attachment. Plump and rotate loose cushions regularly and keep furniture away from prolonged direct sunlight. Follow the instructions supplied with the selected fabric.

Marlow, Calder and Rowan have fixed covers. Alder has a removable, washable cover; the supplied care label determines how it should be washed.

For commercial spaces.

Manolo and Cambridge Linen are not offered for commercial projects. We confirm suitable upholstery and the complete specification for the intended use before agreeing an order. Discuss your project with Hollin & Vale Trade.
