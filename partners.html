Manufacturing partnerships

We are selecting our launch partners.

Hollin & Vale is looking to work with a small number of established manufacturers and trade suppliers that fit the brand's design, service and production standards.

What we're looking for.

We are particularly interested in stock-light and made-to-order arrangements that can support direct customer fulfilment and future own-label development.

Trade pricingClear wholesale structure, delivery costs and volume thresholds.
Direct fulfilmentNeutral or brand-compatible delivery to residential and trade customers.
Commercial capabilityAppropriate upholstery specifications and supporting documentation where relevant.
Brand flexibilityCurated naming, approved imagery and potential for exclusive specifications.
Dependable aftercareTransparent warranty, damage, replacement and lead-time processes.
