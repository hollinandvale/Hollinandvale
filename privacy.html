Privacy

Privacy information.

How Hollin & Vale handles information provided through this pre-launch website and direct enquiries.

Last updated: 29 October 2026.

Hollin & Vale is a furniture brand in development. This website does not currently accept online orders or payments.

Information we may receive

If you contact us by email or use the contact page to prepare an email, you may provide your name, email address, business name and the contents of your message.

How we use it

We use enquiry information to respond to you, discuss potential trade or supplier relationships, keep appropriate correspondence records and take steps you request before any future commercial relationship.

Website data and cookies

We do not currently use advertising cookies, behavioural tracking or ecommerce analytics on this website. Our hosting and network providers may process basic technical information such as IP addresses and server logs to operate and secure the service.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers we use for business email, hosting and document storage where necessary to operate the business. We keep correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary for the enquiry, relationship, record-keeping or legal purpose involved.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights over personal information about you, including access, correction or deletion. To make a privacy request, contact enquiries@hollinandvale.co.uk.

Changes

We will update this notice when the website begins using new services such as ecommerce, analytics, customer accounts or marketing tools.
