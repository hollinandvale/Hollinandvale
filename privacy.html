Privacy

Privacy information.

How Hollin & Vale handles information shared through this retail collection website and direct correspondence.

Last updated: 4 November 2026.

This website displays retail furniture prices and lets you submit enquiries about products, physical fabric samples, delivery, trade projects or aftercare. It does not currently take payments, accept orders online or provide finance applications.

Information you may share

When you contact us, you may provide your name, email address, optional telephone number and preferred contact time, business name, delivery postcode, furniture selection, fabric sample shortlist and message. Submitted enquiries are stored for Hollin & Vale to review and respond to. A reference is shown only after your enquiry has been saved. The email alternative prepares a message in your own email app; you choose whether to send it there.

Furniture and sample selections can appear in shareable page URLs. Personal contact details are submitted in the enquiry request body rather than added to a share link. Avoid putting personal information into a shared product URL.

How we use correspondence

We use information you send to answer questions, discuss and confirm order requests, provide product advice, check delivery requirements, discuss trade or supplier relationships and keep appropriate records. Before an accepted order requires additional information or arrangements, we explain the relevant details.

Saved selections on your device

If you choose “Save selection”, the design, size, fabric and colour are stored in your browser on that device. Contact details and payment information are not included. You can remove individual saved choices or clear the list. A shared configuration link contains product choices only; displayed prices are recalculated from the current catalogue.

Website data and cookies

We do not currently use advertising cookies, behavioural tracking or ecommerce analytics on this website. Hosting and network providers may process basic technical information such as IP addresses, requested URLs and server logs to operate and secure the service. The enquiry service uses short-lived security challenges and request limits to reduce automated abuse. These controls do not create an advertising profile.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Providers of business email, hosting and document storage may process information as needed to run the business. The website enquiry service is hosted using OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare infrastructure; enquiry records are available to Hollin & Vale through authenticated management access and are not published on the website. Where necessary to fulfil an accepted order or handle aftercare, relevant order and delivery information may also be shared with the manufacturer or delivery provider. We retain correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for the enquiry, order, relationship, record-keeping or legal purpose.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights over personal information about you, including access, correction or deletion. For a privacy request, email enquiries@hollinandvale.co.uk.

Changes

We will update this information when new services, such as online payments, customer accounts, analytics or marketing tools, are introduced.
