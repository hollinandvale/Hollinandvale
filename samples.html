    Help choosing a fabric
    Lovely Velvet

    Soft, with a pile.

    Polyester velvet with an EasyClean finish. The direction of the pile and the light in your room affect its appearance.

    Manolo

    A tactile texture.

    Soft polyester chenille, available on Marlow, Calder and Rowan. Follow the supplied care guidance for the chosen fabric.

    Cambridge linen blend

    Natural character.

    43% cotton, 32% linen and 25% polyester. Available across all four designs, including Alder’s removable cover.

    Manolo and Cambridge are not suitable for commercial use in the standard specification. Ask about a trade project so the complete upholstery specification can be checked.
