    How do I request fabric samples?

    Choose up to six colours using the swatches above, then select “Enquire about samples”. Your shortlist is added to the enquiry form for you to review and send. This requests a check of your choices; it is not a confirmed sample order.

    Are physical samples available, and what do they cost?

    We confirm physical sample availability, any sample charge, postage and timing with you before arranging dispatch. Your postal address is requested only when dispatch is agreed.

    Will the fabric look exactly like the screen image?

    The swatch photographs are a guide to texture and colour. Screens, lighting and the direction of a fabric’s pile can affect its appearance. Ask about physical samples to compare with the light and colours in your room. Product photographs show example upholstery and do not change when you select a swatch.

    Which fabrics are available for each design?

    Marlow, Calder and Rowan offer Lovely Velvet, Manolo and Cambridge linen blend. Alder offers Cambridge linen blend. Check your chosen design for the available colours. For a commercial setting, ask about the complete upholstery specification; Manolo and Cambridge are not offered for commercial use in the standard specification.
