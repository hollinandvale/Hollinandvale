Worth taking a little time.

Our upholstery is handmade to order. Allow an estimated 6–8 weeks for production, followed by 1–2 weeks for delivery, subject to materials and order confirmation.

Every sofa and chair uses a solid hardwood frame and sprung base. Construction, cushion fillings and cover details are shown on each design page.
